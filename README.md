# Aero Dental & Comfort Lab

### Patient-Centered Dental Clinic Landing Page

A high-conversion dental clinic website concept designed around three major patient concerns: **trust, treatment anxiety, and pricing uncertainty**.

This project combines UX research, information architecture, interaction design, visual design, and front-end development into a single patient-centered digital experience.

### Core Objective

The primary business objective was to increase appointment bookings while building enough trust and clarity for potential patients before their first visit.

The experience was designed around one principle:

> Reduce uncertainty before asking the patient to book.

The website focuses on:

- Building trust before the first appointment
- Reducing dental anxiety
- Making treatment information easier to understand
- Increasing pricing transparency
- Showing clinical expertise
- Demonstrating treatment outcomes
- Reducing friction in appointment booking
- Supporting organic search through educational content

---

## The Challenge

Dental websites commonly provide services, doctor introductions, testimonials, before/after galleries, and appointment forms. The challenge was to connect these elements to the patient's actual decision-making process.

Patients commonly ask:

- Can I trust this clinic?
- Will the treatment hurt?
- How much will it cost?
- Which doctor should I choose?
- How long will treatment take?
- Is this treatment suitable for me?

The project therefore treats uncertainty as a UX problem rather than only a content problem.

---

## UX Strategy

The experience is built around five principles:

1. **Reduce anxiety** — explain what will happen before the patient arrives.
2. **Increase transparency** — provide useful price ranges and explain cost factors.
3. **Build trust** — show doctors through expertise and clinical philosophy.
4. **Demonstrate results** — present structured patient journeys rather than only before/after images.
5. **Reduce friction** — connect treatment discovery, pricing and booking into one continuous flow.

## Interaction System

The prototype is state-driven rather than a collection of isolated animations.

Core states include:

```text
anxietyMode
activeCategory
activeStep
calculatorSelected
activeCaseStudy
```

For example, activating `anxietyMode` changes relevant content across the page and carries the comfort preference into the booking experience.
