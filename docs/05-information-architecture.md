# 05 — Information Architecture

## Page Structure

The landing page contains the following major sections:

1. Navigation & Global Comfort Switch
2. Hero
3. Treatment Finder
4. Your First Visit
5. Doctor Profiles
6. Case Studies
7. Pricing & Cost Estimator
8. Appointment Booking
9. Searchable FAQ
10. Footer

---

# Section Responsibilities

## 01 — Navigation

Provides:

- Services
- Your First Visit
- Doctors
- Case Studies
- Pricing
- FAQ
- Phone CTA
- Online Booking
- Comfort Mode

---

## 02 — Hero

Primary job:

Communicate the clinic's value proposition and immediately establish a calm, reassuring tone.

Default headline direction:

> Modern dentistry built around your comfort.

Anxiety-active direction:

> Fear-free dentistry. Your comfort is our contract.

---

## 03 — Treatment Finder

Categories:

- General Care
- Cosmetic & Veneers
- Implants & Restorative
- Orthodontics
- Children's Dentistry

Each treatment can expose:

- Treatment name
- Estimated base cost
- Recovery information
- Comfort badge
- Cost estimator action
- Booking action

---

## 04 — Your First Visit

Four stages:

1. Welcome & Dialogue
2. Digital Scan
3. No-Surprises Consultation
4. No-Pressure Decision

---

## 05 — Doctors

Doctor cards contain:

- Name
- Specialty
- Certifications
- Clinical philosophy
- Years of practice
- Clinical focus
- Optional short introduction video

---

## 06 — Case Studies

Three initial case categories:

- Anxiety & Restorative
- Full Smile Redesign
- Pain-Free Implant

Each case communicates:

- Starting condition
- Patient concerns
- Treatment plan
- Outcome
- Recovery information

---

## 07 — Cost Estimator

Users can select treatments and receive an estimated low-to-high range.

The estimator also explains factors affecting the final cost.

---

## 08 — Booking

Four steps:

1. Treatment
2. Practitioner
3. Calendar
4. Contact + Comfort Preferences

---

## 09 — FAQ

FAQ categories:

- Pricing / Insurance
- Anxiety / Sedation
- Emergency Care
- Treatments

---

## 10 — Footer

Contains:

- Utility links
- Location
- Directions
- Opening hours
- Professional credentials
