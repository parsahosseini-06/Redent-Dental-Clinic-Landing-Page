# 01 — Discovery

## Business

**Business Type:** Dental Clinic

### Core Services

- Dental Implants
- Orthodontics
- Root Canal Treatment
- Dental Veneers & Laminates
- Children's Dentistry
- Prosthodontics / Artificial Teeth
- Oral & Maxillofacial Surgery
- Cosmetic Dentistry
- General Dentistry

---

## Business Goal

Provide high-quality dental care using modern technology while creating a comfortable, stress-free experience.

---

## Website Goals

### Primary Goal

Generate more appointment bookings.

### Secondary Goals

- Build trust before the first visit
- Showcase doctors and expertise
- Demonstrate successful treatment outcomes
- Rank on Google's first page for targeted keywords
- Educate potential patients
- Increase phone calls and online reservations

---

## Success Metrics

The original roadmap identified these KPIs:

- Increase booked appointments
- Increase phone calls
- Higher Google rankings
- More organic traffic
- Lower bounce rate
- Higher conversion rate
- More returning visitors

These are target metrics for a deployed product. They should not be presented as measured results unless analytics data is later available.
