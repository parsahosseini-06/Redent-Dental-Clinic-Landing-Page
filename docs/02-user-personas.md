# 02 — User Personas

## Primary Audience

### Demographics

- Age: approximately 25–45
- Income: Middle to upper-middle

### Needs

- Quality treatment
- Beautiful smile
- Modern equipment
- Experienced doctors
- Long-lasting results

## Secondary Audience

Parents looking for children's dental care.

---

# Persona A — The Nervous Patient

## Job To Be Done

> When I need dental work, I want to find a clinic that takes my anxiety seriously and provides a comfortable experience, so that I do not delay treatment because of fear.

### Pain Points

- Fear of pain
- Fear of clinical procedures
- Fear of judgment
- Loud or unfamiliar equipment
- Feeling out of control
- Previous bad experiences

### Design Response

- Global Comfort / Anxiety Mode
- Step-by-step first-visit walkthrough
- Clear sedation information
- Comfort preferences
- Reassuring copy
- Ability to communicate comfort needs during booking

---

# Persona B — The Practical Patient

## Job To Be Done

> When I require clinical care, I want clear cost information and an efficient booking process so that I do not waste time calling clinics or encounter unexpected costs.

### Pain Points

- Hidden fees
- Dental jargon
- Tedious phone booking
- Lack of pricing information

### Design Response

- Interactive Cost Estimator
- Transparent service price ranges
- Treatment Finder
- Four-step booking widget
- Pre-filled booking information

---

# Persona C — The Family Decision Maker

## Job To Be Done

> When I organize dental visits for my family, I want to understand the doctors' qualifications, specialties and approach so that I can feel confident about choosing care.

### Pain Points

- Difficulty verifying expertise
- Impersonal doctor profiles
- Limited information about specialties
- Lack of structured treatment evidence

### Design Response

- Detailed doctor profiles
- Clinical philosophy
- Certifications
- Structured case studies
- Specialty categorization
- Personal introductions
