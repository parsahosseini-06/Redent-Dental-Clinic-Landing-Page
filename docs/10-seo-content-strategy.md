# 10 — SEO & Content Strategy

## Content Strategy

The website should answer four fundamental questions:

### Why this clinic?

Communicate technology, comfort, transparency and patient-first care.

### Why these doctors?

Show specialties, experience, certifications and clinical philosophy.

### Why this treatment?

Explain treatments in patient-friendly language.

### Why now?

Provide clear next steps and a low-friction booking path.

---

# SEO Strategy

SEO topics are organized around high-intent treatment questions.

## Dental Implants

- Implant Cost
- Implant Recovery
- Implant vs Bridge
- Implant Aftercare

## Orthodontics

- Invisalign vs Braces
- Adult Orthodontics
- Treatment Duration

## Children's Dentistry

- First Dental Visit
- Tooth Decay Prevention
- Fluoride Treatment

---

# Content Principles

The strategy avoids publishing content purely for keyword volume.

Useful content formats include:

- Educational articles
- Short videos
- Treatment animations
- Interactive FAQs
- Step-by-step treatment explanations
- Recovery guides

---

# Search Intent

Content should map to different stages of the patient's journey.

```text
Informational
"What is an implant?"
        ↓
Comparative
"Implant vs bridge"
        ↓
Commercial
"Implant cost"
        ↓
Decision
"Book implant consultation"
```

The exact keyword set should be validated against real search data before being used as a production SEO plan.
